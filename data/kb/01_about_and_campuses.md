## Overview
CHRIST (Deemed to be University), also called Christ University, is a deemed-to-be-university located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It was founded in 1969 as Christ College, and the University Grants Commission (UGC) conferred autonomy on the college in 2004. The main campus is the Bangalore Central Campus on Hosur Road.

## Bangalore Central Campus
The Bangalore Central Campus was established in 1969. It is located on Hosur Road near Bangalore Dairy. Address: CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Dharmaram College Post, Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560029, Karnataka, India.

## Bangalore Bannerghatta Road Campus
The Bannerghatta Road (BGR) Campus is in Hulimavu on Bannerghatta Road. It was inaugurated in May 2016. Address: Hulimavu, Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru 560076, Karnataka, India.

## Bangalore Kengeri Campus
The Kengeri Campus is on Mysore Road, about 27 km from the Central Campus. It houses the School of Engineering and Technology and the School of Architecture. Address: Kanmanike, Kumbalgodu, Mysore Road, Bengaluru 560074, Karnataka, India.

## Bangalore Yeshwanthpur Campus
The Yeshwanthpur Campus started functioning in August 2022. It is located at Nagasandra, near Tumkur Road, Bengaluru 560073, Karnataka, India. Students coming by bus can get down at the Nagasandra stop or the HMT Layout stop.

## Campuses outside Bangalore
CHRIST also has a Delhi NCR Campus at Mariam Nagar, Meerut Road, Ghaziabad 201003 (established in 2013), and a Pune Lavasa Campus (established in 2007).
