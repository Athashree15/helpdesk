## What CCHS does
The Centre for Counselling and Health Services (CCHS) supports the emotional wellbeing and academic success of students. Students can meet a counsellor for personal, emotional, academic or career concerns. Counselling is confidential.

## Attendance monitoring and academic support
CCHS motivates students to attend classes regularly and maintain the required attendance of 85% and above. It helps students who are struggling academically or with attendance.

## Parent-faculty meetings
CCHS organises two parent meetings every academic year: one online meeting and one offline meeting.

## How to reach a counsellor
Students can visit the CCHS office on their campus or ask their class teacher to connect them with a counsellor. In an emergency, students should contact campus security or call the national emergency number 112.
