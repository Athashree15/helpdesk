## Minimum attendance requirement
Students must have a minimum of 85% attendance to be permitted to write the End Semester Examinations (ESE). The 85% requirement is a university norm and applies to all programmes.

## When leave applications are accepted
A leave application will be accepted only if the actual attendance of the student is above 75%. Approved leave is considered only if the overall attendance is above 75%.

## Daily sessions and late arrival
The working day has a forenoon session of four periods and an afternoon session of two periods. Attendance is marked at the beginning of each class. A student who arrives late is not given attendance for that period, even if the teacher permits the student to sit in the class.

## Absence and leave procedure
Students may not stay away from classes without prior permission from the Vice Chancellor. Absence for more than two days, for any reason, requires a signed leave form to be submitted within ten days of the leave period. For unforeseen absence, students must apply to the Vice Chancellor as early as possible and not later than three days after returning to college. After leave is approved, the student must submit a form listing the periods missed, signed by the concerned teachers, together with the original leave letter, within three days of approval. The Vice Chancellor may reject a leave application if not satisfied that it is genuine.

## Long absence
Absence of two weeks or more without written permission from the Vice Chancellor is treated as the student having left the university.

## Attendance reports for parents
Students receive periodic test reports covering attendance and academic progress and are responsible for showing them to their parents. Attendance and progress reports are also available online; parents can obtain login details from the IPM office if needed.
