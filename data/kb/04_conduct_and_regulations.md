## Academic materials
Students must bring the required textbooks, laboratory records and instruments to class.

## Clubs, societies and outside commitments
Students need prior permission from the Vice Chancellor to join clubs or societies or to take on commitments that could interfere with their studies. Students cannot play for teams against the university.

## Meetings, notices and collections
Organising or attending meetings, collecting money, circulating notices or petitions, or posting anything on notice boards requires written permission from the Vice Chancellor.

## Political activity
Students should not engage in political activity that conflicts with academic life.

## Complaints and petitions
Students may not file complaints or petitions as a group. Concerns may be presented individually or through proper student representatives.

## Vehicle parking
Students can park four-wheelers inside the university campus only on payment of the prescribed parking fee.

## Anti-ragging
Ragging in any form is prohibited. The university has an Anti Ragging Cell; students who face or witness ragging should report it to the Anti Ragging Cell or to the Office of Student Affairs immediately.

## Dress code
Students are advised and encouraged to follow the dress code specified by the university and their department.
