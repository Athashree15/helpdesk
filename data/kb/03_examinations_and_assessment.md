## Assessment components
Each course is assessed through Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) and an End Semester Examination (ESE). CIA is conducted by the course faculty through the semester and usually has components named CIA 1, CIA 2 and CIA 3 (for example assignments, a mid-semester examination and projects or presentations). CIA marks are evaluated by the course faculty, then reviewed and approved by the Head of Department before they reach the Office of Examinations.

## Weightage
A common pattern is that each course is assessed for 100 marks, with 50% from the ESE and 50% from CIA (in some programmes this is 45% CIA plus 5% for attendance). Some programmes follow a 70:30 assessment pattern. In many programmes the candidate must score a minimum of 50% in the ESE of each course. The exact weightage and pass criteria depend on your programme, so always check your programme handbook or ask your class teacher.

## Eligibility for ESE
To write the End Semester Examination a student must have at least 85% attendance (see the attendance regulations).

## Supplementary examinations
Supplementary examinations give students who were unable to appear for a scheduled End Semester Examination (ESE) an opportunity to complete the missed course(s) in accordance with the university examination regulations. Notifications for supplementary examinations (for example odd semester, even semester and trimester supplementary examinations) are published on the Examination Notifications page of the university website.

## CIA repeat
Students who have backlogs because of low marks in CIA can apply for a CIA Repeat. CIA Repeat notifications are published separately for different programmes (for example LLB, B.Tech/B.Arch, programmes with a 70:30 pattern, and the programme-completed batch). The application form for Repeat CIA Course Admission is available from the Office of Examinations.

## Results and marks cards
Results are published on the university website. Students are instructed to print the marks card on an A4 sheet in portrait format.
