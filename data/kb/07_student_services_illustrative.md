## Student portal
Attendance, CIA marks, timetables and announcements are available on the university student portal (Knowledge Pro). Students log in with their register number. Forgotten passwords are reset through the IT Support desk on campus.

## Library
Each campus has a central library. Students borrow books using their university ID card. Overdue books attract a fine. Library timings and borrowing limits are displayed at the library and on the library web page; check them there because they change during examinations and vacations.

## Wi-Fi and IT support
Campus Wi-Fi is available to students using their university credentials. For login problems, laptop configuration or email issues, visit the IT Support desk on your campus and carry your ID card.

## Hostel accommodation
On-campus hostel accommodation is limited and allotted on application. Separate hostels are provided for men and women. Hostel fees, allotment dates and rules are announced by the hostel office each year. Students who do not get a hostel seat may look for university-recommended paying-guest accommodation.

## Fee payment
Fees are paid online through the payment link published by the Finance Office. Students must pay before the due date shown in the fee notification; late payment attracts a fine. Fee receipts can be downloaded after payment. For fee-related problems, contact the Finance Office of your campus.

## Scholarships and financial aid
The university offers scholarships and fee concessions to eligible students based on merit and financial need. Applications are invited through a notification each year. Students should also check government scholarship portals such as the National Scholarship Portal.

## ID card
Every student must carry the university ID card on campus. A lost ID card should be reported immediately to the Office of Student Affairs, and a duplicate card can be issued on payment of a fee.
